# OpenAI Researcher Access - Route Assessment

> HOLD - DO NOT SUBMIT

- Criteria checked: 2026-07-23
- Official source: <https://openai.com/form/researcher-access-program/>
- Current readiness: **route mismatch / not ready**

The program supports actual research into responsible AI deployment, risk
mitigation, and societal impact. It offers up to $1,000 in API credits for a
12-month research period and reviews applications quarterly.

## Why the current campaign does not qualify as a packet

PermitMesh currently has a product and OSS validation campaign, not a defined
research study. There is no verified:

- research question and preregistered evaluation plan;
- eligible applicant affiliation or research-organization context;
- participant recruitment and consent plan;
- data-management, privacy, or publication plan;
- budget that maps API use to research experiments;
- evidence that the work is research rather than operational product support.

The security subject matter alone does not turn development into research.

## Reopen conditions

This route may be reassessed only if a genuine research project exists. A
future packet must identify:

1. a falsifiable question about agent-authorization behavior;
2. a method, sample, comparison, and success/failure measures;
3. an eligible and accountable applicant;
4. data handling and publication commitments;
5. an API-credit budget tied only to the study;
6. the current official program criteria and deadline.

No application narrative is drafted because doing so now would encourage
retrofitting ordinary product work into a research grant.
