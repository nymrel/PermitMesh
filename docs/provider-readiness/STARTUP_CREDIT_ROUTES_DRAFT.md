## AWS Activate

Official source: <https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/>

AWS currently describes:

- Founders: bootstrapped or self-funded, starting at $1,000 and potentially up
  to $5,000;
- Portfolio: pre-Series B with an AWS Activate Provider Organization ID,
  potentially up to $200,000;
- common conditions including company founded within 10 years, paid-tier AWS
  account, and prior-credit limits.

Current status: **potentially eligible, unverified**.

Required facts:

- legal entity name and founding date;
- current funding stage and provider affiliation;
- prior AWS Activate credits;
- paid-tier AWS account owner and account ID, kept outside this repository;
- intended services, monthly forecast, and data/security boundaries.

## Google for Startups Cloud Program

Official source: <https://cloud.google.com/startup/faq>

Google describes a Start tier for digital-native startups with a working MVP,
clear business model, and plans to seek venture funding, plus a Scale tier for
VC-funded startups. The application requires a Google Cloud billing ID and
business email aligned with the public company domain.

Current status: **potential route, unverified**.

Required facts:

- whether the applying studio entity fits a permitted company type;
- working MVP and business model used in the application;
- funding and venture-intent facts;
- prior Google Cloud credits;
- matching business-domain email and billing account;
- intended architecture and cost forecast.

Do not describe PermitMesh as AI-first merely because AI agents are a user
category; the company must satisfy Google's current AI-first definition if
applying for an AI tier.

## Microsoft for Startups

Official source: <https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups>

Microsoft currently advertises startup credits, AI tools, and development
resources, but exact tier and eligibility details are resolved through the
current program flow and can change.

Current status: **potential route, unverified**.

Required facts:

- current legal-entity and product eligibility;
- funding and investor-referral status;
- prior Microsoft/Azure startup credits and account history;
- eligible tenant/account owner, stored outside this repository;
- Azure services, architecture, monthly forecast, and exit plan.

Refresh the official portal immediately before a readiness ruling. Do not rely
on forum posts or old Founders Hub thresholds.

## Common application record

Before any route advances, create a private operator-reviewed record with:

- legal entity and authorized applicant;
- public company website and matching domain email;
- funding stage and verifiable source;
- prior provider credits;
- account/billing identifiers stored in the approved secret surface;
- workload architecture and 12-month cost forecast;
- why the workload benefits a public project or studio product;
- post-credit sustainability and provider-exit plan;
- exact terms reviewed and date;
- approval, submission, and expiration owners.

No credentials, billing identifiers, or private funding documents belong in
this repository.
