## Published alternative eligibility paths

An applicant can qualify through at least one published path:

- 500 or more dependent repositories;
- 100 or more dependent packages;
- 200,000 or more combined monthly registry downloads;
- listed committer or maintainer of a recognized foundation or language
  project;
- 100 or more merged pull requests into repositories the applicant does not
  own in the last 12 months;
- a maintained repository with 20 or more unique external contributors with
  merged pull requests in the last 12 months;
- OpenSSF criticality score at least 0.4.

Anthropic also allows an explanation for quietly important projects, but that
is not permission to invent ecosystem dependence.
